Home Level 2 chargers can develop fault lights, thermal issues, or communication problems after a few years. Use these questions before you book a mobile diagnostic or repair visit in the Denver area.

## Questions to ask before you book

* **What fault codes or LED patterns is my unit showing?** Note any blinking sequences or color-coded LEDs before resetting the breaker or the technician arrives. Many manufacturers publish error tables (ChargePoint, Qmerit, and similar support guides).
* **Do you work on residential EVSE (Level 2 chargers) specifically?** Ask about experience with common brands, hardwired vs. NEMA plug units, firmware updates, and thermal-trip diagnostics.
* **Will you inspect for thermal stress on plugs and terminals?** Ask them to check NEMA plugs or hardwired terminals for discoloration, burning odors, or excessive heat during high-amp charging sessions.
* **Can you test the dedicated 240V breaker and circuit?** Confirm they will verify ground-fault or thermal trip history and whether the circuit is still adequate for the charger.
* **Are you a licensed electrician for this work?** High-voltage EV supply equipment is not a DIY repair. Ask for license details and whether they carry the right diagnostic tools for EVSE.
* **What is included in a mobile diagnostic visit?** Get the scope and price in writing—trip charge, diagnosis only vs. repair, parts availability, and any follow-up warranty.
* **Will you leave documentation of the fault and the work performed?** Keep records for warranty claims or future service.
