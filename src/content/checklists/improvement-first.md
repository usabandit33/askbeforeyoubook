In Wake County, septic work often requires an Improvement Permit before a Construction Authorization can be issued. The two documents serve different purposes. Starting work with only one of them can delay the project or create compliance issues.

## 1. Understand the two documents

- An **Improvement Permit** evaluates the site and approves the general type and location of the system.
- A **Construction Authorization** allows the actual installation or repair to begin after the Improvement Permit conditions are met.

Ask the contractor and the county which document is required for your specific work and whether both have been obtained.

## 2. Confirm the contractor’s role

Ask:

- Will you obtain the Improvement Permit and Construction Authorization, or is that the homeowner’s responsibility?
- What site information or fees are needed?
- How long does the county typically take to issue each document?

Get the answers in writing so responsibilities are clear.

## 3. Verify before work begins

Do not authorize installation until the required authorizations are in hand. Ask for copies of the approved documents before the contractor mobilizes.
