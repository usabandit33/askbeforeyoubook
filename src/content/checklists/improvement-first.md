In Wake County, septic work often requires an Improvement Permit and a Construction Authorization. Confirming which documents are needed avoids delays.

## 1. Ask which permits apply

Clarify whether the project needs an Improvement Permit, a Construction Authorization, or both before work starts.

## 2. Confirm who pulls the permits

Get in writing whether the company or the homeowner is responsible for filing and fees.

## 3. Verify soil and design steps

Ask whether a soil evaluation or engineered design is required for the proposed system.
