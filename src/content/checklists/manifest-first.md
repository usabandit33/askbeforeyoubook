Grease trap pumping and cleaning must be documented. A manifest or service ticket protects the business and shows compliance.

## 1. Ask for a written manifest

Confirm the company provides a dated receipt or manifest that lists the volume pumped and disposal location.

## 2. Clarify the scope

Get in writing whether the service includes pumping only, cleaning the trap walls, and inspecting the baffles or outlet.

## 3. Confirm frequency and pricing

Ask how often the trap should be serviced for your volume and what the per-visit price includes.
