For horse owners and farm managers. Trailer failures put animals and people at risk, so ask these questions before you book.

## Questions to ask before you book

* **Do you work on horse trailers specifically?** Ask about experience with aluminum and steel trailers, floors, ramps and latches.
* **Can you handle brakes and axles on site?** Ask about electric brakes, breakaway systems, bearings and parts availability.
* **Can you inspect floors under the mats?** Ask whether they pull mats to check for pitting, rot or soft spots.
* **What are your hours and emergency terms?** Ask about weekends, evenings and event days.
* **Are you insured, and what do you warranty?** Ask for details in writing.
* **What will it cost?** Ask for a written estimate, including trip charges, and what happens if more repairs turn up.

## Pre-trip owner checks

Before a trip (or before the mobile tech arrives), check:

* Tire condition and pressure
* Lug nut torque
* Brake and breakaway test
* Floor condition (including under mats when possible)
* Wheel bearing service on the schedule in your trailer manual

Use your owner’s manual and trusted extension or safety sources for exact intervals.

## Why mobile repair can help

Ocala is a major horse community, and trailers are working equipment. When something fails, a repair that comes to the barn can avoid hauling a damaged trailer on the road. Mobile service is not right for every job, so ask what can be done on site and what needs a shop. Also ask whether they work on horse trailers and not only flatbeds or cargo trailers.
