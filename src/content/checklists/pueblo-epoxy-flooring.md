Epoxy and related coatings are used on garage, warehouse, shop, and commercial floors in the Pueblo area. Performance depends more on surface preparation and product choice than on the marketing name. The right system depends on traffic, chemical exposure, temperature swings, and whether the floor sees direct sun.

## 1. Confirm surface preparation and moisture testing

Coatings fail most often from poor prep. Ask:

- Will the concrete be diamond-ground or shot-blasted to a specified profile?
- Has a moisture test (calcium chloride or relative-humidity probe) been performed?
- How will cracks, joints, and oil-contaminated areas be treated?
- Is a moisture-mitigation primer needed?

Get the preparation steps in writing before comparing prices.

## 2. Understand epoxy versus polyaspartic (and topcoats)

- Standard epoxy is durable and chemical-resistant but can yellow or chalk under UV.
- Polyaspartic systems often cure faster, stay more flexible, and handle UV better.
- Clear UV-stable topcoats can be added over a base coat for sun-exposed areas.

Ask which specific products are proposed and request data sheets. Confirm cure time before the floor can be returned to service.

## 3. Compare warranty and expected use

Ask what the warranty covers (peeling, delamination, wear) and for how long. A lower price may use a thinner film or skip proper mechanical preparation.
