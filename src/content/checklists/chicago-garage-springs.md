## Quick answer
When a garage door spring breaks in Chicago, ask whether the company replaces **both** springs, whether the trip/diagnostic fee is credited toward the repair, and who will actually show up.

## Checklist
1. **Will you replace both springs?** Springs work as a pair. Replacing only one often leads to the second one failing soon after.
2. **Is the trip / service fee credited** if I approve the repair?
3. **Who is coming?** Employee or subcontractor?
4. **What warranty do you give on the springs and labor?**
5. **Do you have a phone and website** I can find on your own site?

## Notes
Customer never pays the directory. Call shops that publish their own contact information.
