Synthetic turf is popular in Bend for lower water use and outdoor living spaces. Price differences often come from base preparation, drainage, product quality, and warranty—not just square footage. The useful questions are what goes under the turf, how water will drain, and which product specs and warranty are included.

## 1. Confirm base preparation and drainage

A stable, well-draining base matters as much as the turf face. Ask:

- How deep will the existing material be removed or graded?
- What base material and depth are specified?
- How will water drain away from hardscapes and the house?
- Is a weed barrier or edge restraint included?

Poor prep leads to uneven surfaces and early failure. Get the preparation steps in writing before comparing per-square-foot prices.

## 2. Compare product specifications, not just the name

Ask for the product data sheet: pile height, face weight, backing type, and whether the turf is designed for residential yards, pets, or high traffic. Confirm UV resistance suitable for Central Oregon sun and any heat or pet-related product claims.

Request a sample and a written list of what is included (turf, infill, seaming, cleanup).

## 3. Clarify warranty and maintenance

Ask what the warranty covers (fading, seams, workmanship) and for how long, and what maintenance is required to keep it valid. Confirm whether the quote includes removal of old sod or hardscape, permits if any, and final cleanup.
