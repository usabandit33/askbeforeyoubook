Walk-in cooler problems are often blamed on the compressor when the real issue is a dirty condenser, failed gasket, or refrigerant leak. Confirming the diagnosis first saves money.

## 1. Ask what has been checked

Before approving a compressor replacement, ask whether the condenser coils were cleaned, door gaskets inspected, and a leak search performed.

## 2. Get the diagnosis in writing

Request a clear statement of the failure mode and why other causes were ruled out.

## 3. Compare repair versus replace options

Ask for the cost of repairing the identified issue versus replacing the compressor, and the expected life of each option.

## Before you book

Use the matching Philadelphia walk-in checklist to require a documented diagnosis before authorizing major component replacement.
