Window film performance is defined by visible light transmission (VLT), solar heat gain, and reflectance. Marketing names are not enough.

## 1. Ask for the product data sheet

Request the manufacturer’s specifications for VLT, total solar energy rejected, and reflectance before comparing products.

## 2. Confirm application and warranty

Get in writing which windows will be filmed, the film brand and series, and the warranty terms (including peeling and discoloration).

## 3. Clarify interior versus exterior film

Ask whether the film is applied inside or outside and how that affects durability in Phoenix sun.
