Severe storms, falling branches, and break-ins can leave Little Rock homes and businesses with broken windows or damaged doors. Boarding up exposed openings protects the property from weather and unauthorized entry while permanent glass or door work is arranged. The useful questions are whether the company can dispatch quickly, what the emergency service costs, and whether they can also handle permanent repairs.

## 1. Confirm 24-hour emergency dispatch

Damage rarely happens during business hours. Ask whether the company responds overnight, on weekends, and during severe weather—and whether someone can actually send a crew, not only take a message.

Request a realistic arrival estimate for your address and property type (house, storefront, or large commercial opening).

## 2. Get a clear emergency price breakdown

Ask for the dispatch fee, labor, materials, after-hours charges, and any minimum. A written or verbal breakdown before the crew is sent makes it easier to compare providers without surprise add-ons during a storm surge.

Confirm they have materials suited to your opening size and type.

## 3. Ask about permanent repairs after the board-up

Boarding is temporary. Ask whether the same company provides glass replacement, door repair, or restoration, or can coordinate with another qualified contractor. Clarify what the board-up quote does and does not include for follow-up work.
