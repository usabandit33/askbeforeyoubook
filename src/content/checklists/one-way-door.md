Wildlife in the attic is often removed with a one-way door so animals leave and cannot re-enter. Trapping and exclusion work differently.

## 1. Ask about the removal method

Confirm whether the company uses a one-way door, live traps, or other methods, and how long the door stays in place.

## 2. Clarify exclusion and cleanup

Get in writing whether entry points will be sealed after removal and whether droppings or insulation cleanup is included.

## 3. Confirm licensing and guarantees

Ask about wildlife control licensing and any guarantee against re-entry.
