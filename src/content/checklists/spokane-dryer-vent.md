Older homes in Spokane often have long or winding dryer vent runs. Lint buildup reduces efficiency and raises fire risk. Many jobs only need a thorough clean from the dryer to the exterior termination. Damaged, crushed, or flexible foil/vinyl duct may need replacement or a rigid metal reroute. The right first step is to clean and inspect before approving a full line replace.

## 1. Start with a full lint clean and inspection

Ask whether the service includes cleaning the entire run—not only the dryer end—and checking the exterior termination. A complete clean often restores airflow if the duct is intact.

Request a clear description of access points and any limitations (walls, floors, or multi-story runs).

## 2. Decide when replacement or rigid reroute is needed

Ask what material is in place (foil, vinyl, or rigid/semi-rigid metal). Foil and vinyl collect lint more easily; rigid metal is preferred for safety and airflow.

Confirm whether a shorter or straighter rigid path is recommended and whether that work is priced separately from the clean.

## 3. Get scope, price, and safety notes in writing

Clarify whether the quote is clean-only or includes materials and labor for a new line. Ask about fire-safety guidance the company follows and any warranty on the work.

## Before you book

Use the matching Spokane dryer-vent checklist to require a full clean-and-inspect before authorizing line replacement or a rigid reroute.
