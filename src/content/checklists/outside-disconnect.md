An outdoor emergency disconnect for HVAC or other equipment is often required by code. Confirming the requirement and scope before work starts avoids rework.

## 1. Ask whether a disconnect is required

Confirm the local code requirement for an outdoor disconnect for the equipment being installed or replaced.

## 2. Clarify the scope

Get in writing whether the quote includes the disconnect, wiring, and any panel or breaker changes.

## 3. Verify licensing and inspection

Ask whether the electrician is licensed and whether the work will be inspected.

Use the matching Minneapolis electrical checklist to confirm disconnect requirements and scope before authorizing the job.
