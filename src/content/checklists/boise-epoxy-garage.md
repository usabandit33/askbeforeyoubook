Garage floor coatings in Boise are sold as epoxy, polyaspartic, and hybrid systems. Performance depends more on surface preparation and product choice than on the marketing name. The right system depends on traffic, moisture in the slab, temperature swings, and whether the floor sees direct sun. Compare prep and data sheets before comparing square-foot prices.

## 1. Confirm surface preparation and moisture testing

Coatings fail most often from poor prep. Ask:

- Will the concrete be diamond-ground or shot-blasted to a specified profile?
- Has a moisture test been performed?
- How will cracks, joints, and oil stains be treated?
- Is a moisture-mitigation primer needed?

Get the preparation steps in writing before comparing bids.

## 2. Understand epoxy versus polyaspartic (and topcoats)

- Standard epoxy is durable and chemical resistant but can yellow under UV.
- Polyaspartic systems often cure faster, stay more flexible, and handle UV better.
- Clear UV-stable topcoats can be added over a base coat for sun-exposed areas.

Ask which specific products are proposed and request data sheets. Confirm cure time before the floor can be used.

## 3. Compare warranty and what’s included

Ask what the warranty covers (peeling, delamination, wear) and for how long. Confirm whether the quote includes prep, materials, labor, and cleanup—or only the coating layer. A lower price may use a thinner film or skip mechanical preparation.
