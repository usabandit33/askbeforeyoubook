## Questions to ask before you book

* **Do you hydro-jet the outlet lines and interceptor walls, or only pump the trap?** Ask whether the service includes high-pressure water jetting to strip hardened FOG from internal drain walls, not just liquid removal.
* **How often should we schedule jetting in addition to regular pumping?** Many operators combine pumping with jetting on a set interval (for example every six months); ask for a recommendation based on your kitchen volume.
* **Will you inspect outlet sanitary tees and baffles?** Confirm they check for calcified grease buildup that can cause sudden kitchen backups.
* **Do you use or recommend chemical degreasers?** Many municipal FOG programs restrict or prohibit harsh chemical drain openers that push grease downstream or damage seals. Confirm against Tampa / Hillsborough County guidance.
* **Are you equipped and permitted for commercial grease waste lines?** Ask about licensing, disposal, and whether they handle restaurant interceptors specifically.
* **What documentation will I receive?** Keep dated service logs of pumping and jetting dates for health and environmental inspections.
* **What is the price and emergency availability?** Get the scope in writing, including after-hours or emergency rates if drains are already slow or backing up.

## Preventing kitchen floods with commercial hydro-jetting in Tampa

Tampa’s booming dining and entertainment districts put intense pressure on commercial kitchen drainage. While standard grease interceptor pumping removes floating waste and bottom sludge, it often leaves behind a stubborn layer of grease coating the interior walls of outlet pipes. Over time, this buildup narrows the pipe diameter until a complete blockage occurs during a peak service period.

Standard plumbing companies often avoid greasy commercial waste lines due to the messy nature of the work. When slow drains threaten to shut down a kitchen or trigger health-inspector warnings, restaurant managers need immediate, heavy-duty intervention rather than another routine pump-out.

Specialized commercial hydro-jetting services address this directly. By using high-pressure water streams to scour grease down to bare pipe walls, these specialists restore full drainage capacity, protect kitchens from catastrophic backups, and help operators stay compliant with municipal environmental codes. Pair scheduled pumping with periodic jetting, keep clear service records, and confirm current Tampa / Hillsborough County FOG expectations before you sign a contract.
