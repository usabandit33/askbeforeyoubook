## Quick answer
You need a roll-off for a cleanout or remodel. Call a local yard, ask how many *tons* come with the price, and keep the can on your driveway if you can.

## Austin roll-off checklist
1. **Where will it sit?** Driveway or private pad: usually no City of Austin permit. Street: you need a TURP permit.
2. **How many tons are included?** Say this out loud before you give a card.
3. **What happens if you go over?** Ask overage per ton and the extra-day price.
