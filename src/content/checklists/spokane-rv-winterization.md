Eastern Washington freezes can damage water left in RV plumbing, pumps, heaters, and fittings. Professional winterization prepares motorhomes and travel trailers for storage before sustained cold arrives. The useful questions are what procedures are included, whether mobile service is available, and how the quote compares to a basic blowout alone.

## 1. Confirm what the winterization includes

Ask specifically about:

- Freshwater lines, faucets, valves, and low-point drains
- Water pump and toilet protection
- Water heater drain or bypass per manufacturer guidance
- Exterior faucets, outdoor showers, and other accessories

Request whether the tech uses compressed air, RV-safe antifreeze, or both. Automotive antifreeze is not a substitute for products intended for RV potable-water systems.

## 2. Decide on shop versus mobile service

Mobile winterization can be done at home or storage and may include a travel fee. Confirm the service area, appointment window, and whether RV size or plumbing layout changes the price. Mention unusual plumbing or residential-style appliances when requesting a quote.

## 3. Compare scope, not only the lowest price

Get an itemized quote and ask whether the price covers the full freshwater system or only a limited blowout. Book before the seasonal rush; last-minute appointments are harder once freezing weather is already here.
