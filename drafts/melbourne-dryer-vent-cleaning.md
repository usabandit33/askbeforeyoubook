Melbourne's warm, humid climate can make laundry a year-round chore, but a dryer that suddenly needs longer cycles is not just an inconvenience. Restricted airflow can leave lint in the exhaust path and cause a dryer to overheat. The City of Melbourne Fire Prevention Office says lint accumulation is the leading cause of clothes-dryer fires and recommends cleaning the vent pipe at least annually, or sooner when drying takes longer than normal. That makes the important booking question simple: will the technician clean and check the entire exhaust route, not just the short hose behind the machine?

## 1. Will you clean the entire vent run?

- **Ask:** *"Will your service clean from the dryer connection all the way to the exterior termination, including bends and the outside hood?"*
- **Red flag:** A quote that covers only the lint screen, the short transition hose, or a quick vacuum at the wall opening.

A proper service should identify the route, access points, approximate length, number of turns, and where the exhaust exits. Some Melbourne homes have laundry rooms on interior walls, long duct runs, or vents that terminate through a roof. Those layouts can require different tools and access planning. Ask whether the quoted price includes the full run and whether difficult roof access, a second story, or a disconnected duct would cost extra.

## 2. How will you protect the dryer and the duct?

- **Ask:** *"What do you disconnect, what do you protect, and how do you make sure the duct is reconnected correctly?"*
- **Red flag:** A technician who cannot explain how they will avoid crushing flexible duct, damaging the exterior hood, or leaving the dryer disconnected.

The City of Melbourne recommends checking that the vent behind the dryer is not damaged, crushed, or restricted. It also recommends rigid or flexible metal venting material to support airflow and proper drying time. Ask the technician to point out crushed sections, plastic or foil-like transition material, loose joints, disconnected ducts, or a damaged exterior flap. A cleaning is not a durable fix if the duct is still crushed or improperly routed. If a replacement is needed, get a separate written price and the reason for it.

## 3. Will you verify airflow after cleaning?

- **Ask:** *"Do you test airflow or confirm that the outside vent opens and exhaust is moving after the cleaning?"*
- **Red flag:** The job ends with a pile of lint removed but no check that the dryer is exhausting properly.

The city advises homeowners to check that the outdoor vent covering opens while the dryer operates. A professional should be able to explain what they checked and whether airflow improved. Ask for before-and-after photos where practical, especially if the duct has a long run or hard-to-see sections. Photos are useful evidence, but they are not a substitute for a functional check.

## 4. What happens if you find a damaged duct, bird nest, or unsafe installation?

- **Ask:** *"Will you stop and show me the issue before doing extra work, and will you quote the repair separately?"*
- **Red flag:** An unexpected repair charge or replacement recommendation without photos, an explanation, or your approval.

Lint is not the only possible obstruction. Exterior hoods can stick, screens can collect lint, and animals may nest in a termination. The technician should explain whether the issue is a cleaning problem, a duct-repair problem, or a condition that needs a qualified contractor. Do not accept a promise that a chemical treatment or fragrance will solve restricted airflow. The goal is a clear, unobstructed exhaust path.

## 5. Are you insured, experienced with my vent layout, and clear about the price?

- **Ask:** *"Are you insured, do you service roof exits and long duct runs, and is this a flat price or an estimate?"*
- **Red flag:** A vague quote that excludes common access conditions or an insistence on immediate add-ons.

Give the company the home's approximate age, dryer location, number of stories, and whether the vent exits through a wall or roof. Ask what the quoted service includes, what could change the price, and whether there is a service minimum. If the technician identifies a gas connection concern, damaged wiring, or a problem inside the dryer cabinet, ask whether a separate qualified appliance professional is needed. The City of Melbourne recommends professional installation and servicing, and says gas dryers should be inspected by a qualified professional for intact, leak-free gas connections.

## Before you book

Choose a company that cleans the complete exhaust path, protects and reconnects the duct correctly, checks the exterior termination and airflow, documents damage, and gets approval before extra repairs. Clean the lint filter before or after every load, keep the area around the dryer clear, and stop using the machine if you notice burning smells, unusual heat, or a serious airflow problem. For Melbourne-specific safety guidance, see the city's [dryer fire-prevention tips](https://www.melbourneflorida.org/Government/Departments/Fire-Department/Fire-Prevention-Office/Fire-Prevention-Safety-Tips/Safety-Tips-to-Prevent-Clothes-Dryer-Fires).
